My Treatment

Health payments · Fintech · Mobile

Planning and paying for treatment — with trust at every step.

Overview

My Treatment is a mobile fintech flow for estimating, planning and paying for medical treatment. Money and health are two of the most emotional things we handle, so the entire journey had to feel clear, calm and fair.

Challenge

The core friction was an information gap: patients rarely knew what treatment would cost, how to split it, or what happened next. Estimates were opaque and instalment journeys were littered with drop-off points.

Outcome

We simplified estimate-to-instalment journeys into a legible, step-by-step flow and reinstated trust in an emotionally loaded moment — raising completed sign-ups by 31% after launch.
