Figam

Design collaboration · SaaS tooling · Web

A design-tooling workspace where versioning, reviewing and handoff finally feel calm.

Overview

Figam is a collaboration layer for teams that live inside design tools. The brief was to untangle the messy middle of product design — version chaos, review ping-pong and fragile handoffs — into one quiet workspace designers actually enjoy opening.

Challenge

Teams were drowning in thread comments, orphaned versions and stale design-to-dev specs. Every change spawned review rounds; every review spawned drift. We needed a system that made the current truth obvious and the next action impossible to miss.

Outcome

We rebuilt reviewing, versioning and handoff around progressive disclosure and calm defaults. The result was an interface layer that cut designer-developer round-trips by 40% and made handoff feel like a formality instead of a project phase.
