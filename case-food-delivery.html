Food Delivery

Consumer ordering · UX & UI design · Mobile

A food-ordering app designed for speed and appetite.

Overview

A mobile food-ordering experience redesigned around two urges: deciding fast and checking out faster. Clearer menu hierarchy and a one-thumb checkout flow turned hungry scrolling into completed orders.

Challenge

Long menus, noisy cards and a multi-step checkout diluted appetite at the exact moment people were ready to commit. Every extra tap was a meal lost.

Outcome

By tightening the menu hierarchy and compressing checkout to one hand, order completion rose 26% across test markets.
