Fresh Groceries

E-commerce UX · Product Discovery · Cart & Checkout

A grocery-shopping experience built for fast discovery and a frictionless checkout.

Overview

Fresh Groceries is a simple grocery-shopping experience designed around one job: help people find what they need, trust what they pick, and check out without friction. Every screen — search, aisle, cart — was shaped to keep the weekly shop short.

Challenge

Dense catalogues, inconsistent product imagery and a multi-step cart made routine shopping feel like work. Shoppers hesitated over substitutions and delivery details, and that hesitation showed up as abandoned baskets.

Outcome

Clearer product cards, category-first discovery and a compressed cart-to-door flow helped users make confident choices quickly — cutting checkout friction and lifting completed orders.
