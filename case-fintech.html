Fintech Dashboard

Financial platform · UX & product design · Web

Onboarding, portfolios and reporting — rebuilt around progressive disclosure.

Overview

End-to-end product design for a financial platform. From first login to weekly reporting, everything needed to support confident decisions without overwhelming the people making them.

Challenge

New users met a wall of numbers on day one, and onboarding drop-off was steep. The platform had powerful data but poor hierarchy — insight existed, but nobody could find it quietly.

Outcome

We rebuilt onboarding, portfolio views and reporting around progressive disclosure, lifting retention and cutting onboarding drop-off nearly in half.
